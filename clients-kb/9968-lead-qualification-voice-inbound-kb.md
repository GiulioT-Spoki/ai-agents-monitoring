## Qualification rules (BANT — use before booking)

The caller is **qualified** only if all of the following are true:

1. **Authority:** real business (not personal/consumer only) and the caller decides on or strongly influences this kind of purchase (owner, manager, marketing, sales, ops, IT). If they only gather information for others with no influence, they are not a fit.
2. **Need:** need matches Spoki — inbound chat/voice automation, lead capture, or customer support on messaging. Pure custom ERP/agency development or hardware is not a fit.
3. **Timeline:** plan to start within **90 days** (or “as soon as possible”). Timeline beyond **6 months** with no near-term project → not a fit.
4. **Budget:** soft budget at least **€200/month**, or they accept a discovery call without disclosing budget. Explicit budget under **€100/month** and no willingness to talk to sales → not a fit. Undisclosed budget alone does **not** disqualify.

Mark **not a fit** if any of the following is true:

- Personal / consumer use only
- Asks only for a free chatbot with no business context
- Timeline beyond 6 months with no near-term project
- Explicit budget under €100/month and no willingness to talk to sales
- Request is pure development agency work, custom ERP, or hardware

If information is incomplete, keep asking one question at a time. Do not invent a decision.
