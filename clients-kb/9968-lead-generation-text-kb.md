# ACME SRL — Knowledge base (lead generation demo)

Demo KB for the template agent `[Template] Text — Lead Generation` (account 9968). Facts only.

## Company

- Legal name: ACME SRL
- What we sell: B2B software for customer messaging and AI assistants (WhatsApp-first)
- Target: SMEs and mid-market companies in Italy and Spain that talk to customers on chat/phone
- We do **not** sell hardware, consumer apps, or one-off freelance consulting

## Qualification rules (use before tagging)

Mark **qualified** only if all of the following are true:

1. Company is a real business (not a private individual shopping for personal use)
2. Contact has a decision or strong influence role (owner, manager, marketing, sales, ops, IT)
3. Need matches our offer: inbound chat/voice automation, lead capture, or customer support on messaging
4. Timeline to start is within **90 days** (or “as soon as possible”)
5. Soft budget: they can invest at least **€200/month**, or they accept an intro call without disclosing budget

Mark **not a fit** if any of the following is true:

- Personal / consumer use only
- Asks only for a free chatbot with no business context
- Timeline beyond **6 months** with no near-term project
- Explicit budget under **€100/month** and no willingness to talk to sales
- Request is pure development agency work, custom ERP, or hardware

If information is incomplete, keep asking one question at a time. Do not invent a decision.

## Soft budget guidance

- Ask once, without pressure
- If they decline: record budget as undisclosed and continue qualification on the other criteria
- Undisclosed budget alone does **not** make them not a fit

## Product fit (short)

We can help with:

- WhatsApp inbound qualification and handoff to sales
- FAQ and support deflection with a human fallback
- Voice agents for callbacks and appointment-related flows (sold separately; this chat agent only qualifies interest)

We cannot:

- Guarantee lead volume or conversion rates
- Integrate arbitrary undocumented legacy systems in the first call
- Provide legal or medical advice

## Pricing (public range for FAQ only)

- Exact quote only after a sales call — do not invent a custom price
